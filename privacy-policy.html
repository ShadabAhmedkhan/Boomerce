Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

Boomerce ("we", "us", "our") operates boomerce.com and related products including StockPro, EMS, and RenewedDubai (together, the "Services"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our Services, you agree to the practices described here.

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, company, email address, phone number, and project details when you submit a contact form or request a demo. We also collect information automatically, such as IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, through standard web technologies and cookies.

We use the information we collect to respond to inquiries, provide and improve our products and services, process transactions, send relevant updates, and maintain the security and performance of our Services. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Cookies

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.
